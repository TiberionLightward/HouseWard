House Ward

WoW Forever · Roleplaying Ruleset Guild

“Different paths, one watch.”

Founding Creed

House Ward began as a small human household dedicated to protecting travellers, settlements, and ancient places that larger armies overlooked. Its members believe that protection takes many forms: shield and steel, healing and faith, vigilance in the wild, knowledge of dangerous magic, and even a carefully placed dagger.

“Different paths, one watch.”

The Name-Earning Tradition

Those who formally join surrender their former surname and receive a new name ending in Ward. The first part reflects the duty, talent, or personal quality through which they serve the House. This ensures the Ward is not merely a bloodline, but a sworn home accepting souls from all races, cultures, and walks of life.

Alliance Retinue

Nine sworn members stand watch for the Alliance, each bearing a name earned through service to House Ward.

One Watch, Many Duties

House Ward is a sworn household, not a bloodline. Its members come from different peoples and callings, bound by earned trust and the promise that the vulnerable should never stand alone.

Field partners

Garrick Wildward is Tiberion Lightward’s trusted scout. Vaelira Duskward works beside him as an infiltrator and intelligence gatherer.

Checks and balances

Lucian Gloomward and Shalindra Starward challenge one another’s judgement. Her counsel helps keep his dangerous magical studies accountable.

Steel and storm

Thoric Steelward brought Thorgun Stormward into the House. They still tell very different stories about the mining-site disturbance that first brought them together.

Trust across peoples

Lunaria Moonward was the first Night Elf to trust the House fully. Aetheria Spellward maintains its magical equipment and regularly debates Thoric about enchantments.

Swear the Oath

House Ward is an Alliance-only guild on the WoW Forever roleplaying ruleset. There is no online application: membership is discussed in character, in World of Warcraft.

A required part of joining Every accepted character must follow the House naming tradition and take a personal surname ending in “Ward” before joining the guild. Characters who do not adopt a Ward-name cannot join House Ward.
  1. When one of the nine founding members is online, send them a written in-game message to request an interview.
  2. Arrange a time to meet at the Lion’s Pride Inn in Goldshire. Share your character’s background and why they wish to join.
  3. If you are welcomed into the House, agree on the Ward-name your character will earn. You must adopt this name to join the guild; your character can then be added to the retinue.

Need help creating your character’s personal lore or choosing a fitting Ward-name? You are welcome to ask any of the nine founding members for guidance during your interview.

Example message to request an interview

“Greetings, [founding member]. I am [character name], an Alliance [race] [class], and I would like to learn more about joining House Ward. Would you be available to meet for an interview at the Lion’s Pride Inn in Goldshire? I’m happy to arrange a time that suits you.”

Example words for the interview

“I, [character name], offer my skills and loyalty to House Ward. I will stand watch beside my sworn kin and protect those who cannot protect themselves. Different paths, one watch.”

These are examples, not required scripts. Make the words your character’s own and speak with whichever founding member is available.
